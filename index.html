Everything you need to animate.

  • 120+ Effects

    Animoia ships with more than 120 built-in effects, from blurs, glows, and color tools to glitches, distortions, and stylized looks. Every effect is layer based, fully keyframeable, and can be stacked and combined to build your own custom looks.

  • Tracking & Stabilization

    Animoia features built-in tracking and stabilization tools that let you track motion in your footage, attach elements to moving objects, and stabilize shaky shots for smoother results. With a rebuilt tracking system designed to improve reliability and accuracy, you can integrate visual elements naturally.

  • Color Grading

    Shape the mood of your footage with curves, levels, color wheels, and LUT support. Grade individual layers or entire compositions, and preview every adjustment in real time so your colors land exactly where you want them.

  • Keyframes & Modifiers

    Animate anything with a fast, familiar timeline. Set keyframes on any property, adjust easing, and layer on modifiers like wiggle, loop, and repeaters to build complex motion without writing a single expression.

  • Text Animation

    Bring type to life with per-character animators, presets, and full Unicode support. Animate position, scale, opacity, and color across letters, words, or lines, and combine animators for kinetic typography in seconds.

  • Graph Editor

    Fine tune every motion with a full graph editor. Drag bezier handles, compare curves across properties, and sculpt speed and value graphs so your animations feel exactly as smooth or as snappy as you intend.

  • Pen Tool

    Draw masks, shapes, and motion paths directly on the canvas with a precise pen tool. Animate strokes, trim paths, and attach layers to your paths to create write-on effects and custom motion trajectories.

  • Puppet Pin

    Add pins to any layer and bend, stretch, and deform it naturally. Puppet Pin builds a mesh around your artwork so you can bring characters and cutouts to life with organic, keyframeable movement.

  • Rigid Bodies

    Simulate real physics inside your compositions. Give layers mass, bounce, and friction, add gravity and collisions, and let Animoia calculate believable motion that would take hours to keyframe by hand.

Built by one person who loves motion.

Hi, I'm the developer behind Animoia. I spent years working in motion graphics and compositing, and kept running into the same wall: the tools I relied on were either expensive, locked behind subscriptions, or missing the flexibility I wanted for my own workflow.

So I started building my own. Animoia began as a personal project and has grown into a full motion graphics and compositing app with a layer based timeline, keyframes, a graph editor, and GPU accelerated effects. It is completely free, and I intend to keep it that way.

Every feature is shaped by feedback from the community. If you have an idea, hit a bug, or just want to share what you made, come say hello on Discord.
